How to Play

  • Every arrow points its way out. Tap one to send it off the board in the direction it faces.
  • The path must be clear. If another arrow sits between it and the edge, it won't move — clear a way first.
  • Stuck? Hint glows a move that's ready right now. Undo steps back, Reset starts the puzzle over.
  • Fewer moves, higher score. Every attempted tap counts — including blocked ones and undos. Solve it clean for a 3-star run; fumble around and the score drops. No clock, just precision.
