Admin & Operations

"We don't ask for charity. We ask for a share in the work."

All donations to Rotaract Club of Coimbatore are routed through our sponsoring Rotary club's registered account and recorded in our monthly bulletin. Receipts are issued on request, and our treasurer shares a full spend report at every Annual General Meeting. For donations above ₹10,000, or for corporate CSR partnerships, reach out directly at hello@rotaractcoimbatore.org.
