Who we are

About Us

We're a club of students and young professionals who believe service works better when it's done together. Since our charter, we've run health camps, taught in under-resourced schools, planted forests, and built friendships that outlast any single project. Every member brings a different skill — we just point them at the same problems.

The name, explained
Ro·ta·ract Rotary + Interact — a Rotary-sponsored club for people aged 18–30, built on leadership, fellowship, and hands-on service.

A short history

Our club was instituted in our college premises by the initiative of our honorable Principal and Secretary, Dr.V.Sengodan. Our club has the privilege of being the 1s club in the history of Rotaraction in the RI District 3200, of being installed by the Rotary Governor. This club was charted on October 6, 2006. Our first President was Rtr. M.Karunakaran, followed by Rtr. Murali Krishna, who led the club for the second year. Our club has won many individual awards at district fellowship, group III forum and all other events. We organized several events for the welfare of the society and the members of the club. We look forward in continuing our good show in the future also.

Our Story, in Full

What started as a handful of college friends volunteering on weekends grew into a chartered club with its own board, budget, and calendar of projects. We work across four focus areas — health, education, environment, and community development — and we run on the same principle every year: identify a real gap, then close it, together. New members don't wait for seniority to lead something; the newest inductee can chair a project in their very first term.
